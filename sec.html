About EduHome Malawi

EduHome Malawi is a free, inclusive digital learning platform built to support secondary school students across the country. We believe that quality education should not be limited by geography, economic status, or access to physical resources.

Our curriculum is fully aligned with the Malawi National Curriculum, offering detailed notes, video tutorials, and interactive quizzes for Form 1 through Form 4. Whether you are revising for MSCE or learning a new topic for the first time, EduHome gives you the tools to succeed at your own pace, right from home.
